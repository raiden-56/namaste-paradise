About us

Water sports and mini cruises from Tadadi

We share the joy of the sea along the coastline of Gokarna and Paradise Beach.

Our story

The thrill of the sea, the calm of the coast

Namaste Paradise Cruise & Watersports runs mini cruises and water sports from Tadadi, where the Aghanashini river meets the Arabian Sea. Our trips combine the excitement of the water with the quiet beauty of Gokarna’s coves — Paradise, Half Moon and Om Beach.

On board, our crew explain each point along the way, and our instructors run kayaking, snorkeling and water sports with safety gear for every guest. The cruise boat has its own restroom, and our base has changing rooms.

Booking stays simple and personal. Message us on WhatsApp or call — our office is open 24×7 — and we will welcome you at our counter near Union Bank, Tadadi.

Three things we hold to

  1. 01

    The sea sets the schedule

    We sail when it is right to sail. Weather and sea conditions always come before timetables.

  2. 02

    Clear from the first message

    Fares, timing and boarding details on WhatsApp before you confirm. No surprises.

  3. 03

    We keep our distance from wildlife and leave beaches as we found them.

The coastline

Gokarna’s coast, stop by stop

From the river mouth at Tadadi, the coast runs north past secluded coves to the temple town. Every route is confirmed with you before you sail.
  1. 01Tadadi
  2. 02Paradise
  3. 03Half Moon

  1. Stop 01 · Where every trip begins

    Aghanashini river and Tadadi

    On our route

    South of the beaches, the Aghanashini river meets the Arabian Sea through mangroves and fishing villages. Our counter is at Tadadi, by the fishing harbour and the river mouth, and every trip starts here.

    Fishing harbour, river backwaters and the lighthouse, all in the first minutes of the ride.

    Start of every trip.

  2. Stop 02 · Rocks, palms, solitude

    Paradise Beach

    On our route

    Further along the cliffs, a rugged little beach of boulders and pale sand. Remote by land, it rewards those who approach it slowly from the water.

    One of the most secluded stretches on this coast, framed by dark rock and green headland.

    On the premium beach-hopping route.

  3. Stop 03 · A quiet crescent

    Half Moon Beach

    On our route

    A small, sheltered cove beneath a wooded headland, reached on foot by a coastal trail or in a single sweep from the sea. Guests on our premium trip usually stop here to swim, kayak and snorkel.

    One of the beaches approved for water activities, with forest running almost to the waterline.

    Usual activity stop on the premium route, subject to conditions.
