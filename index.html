The coastline

Gokarna’s coast, stop by stop

From the river mouth at Tadadi, the coast runs north past secluded coves to the temple town. Every route is confirmed with you before you sail.
  1. 01Tadadi
  2. 02Paradise
  3. 03Half Moon
  4. 04Om
  5. 05Kudle
  6. 06Main

  1. Stop 01 · Where every trip begins

    Aghanashini river and Tadadi

    On our route

    South of the beaches, the Aghanashini river meets the Arabian Sea through mangroves and fishing villages. Our counter is at Tadadi, by the fishing harbour and the river mouth, and every trip starts here.

    Fishing harbour, river backwaters and the lighthouse, all in the first minutes of the ride.

    Start of every trip.

  2. Stop 02 · Rocks, palms, solitude

    Paradise Beach

    On our route

    Further along the cliffs, a rugged little beach of boulders and pale sand. Remote by land, it rewards those who approach it slowly from the water.

    One of the most secluded stretches on this coast, framed by dark rock and green headland.

    On the premium beach-hopping route.

  3. Stop 03 · A quiet crescent

    Half Moon Beach

    On our route

    A small, sheltered cove beneath a wooded headland, reached on foot by a coastal trail or in a single sweep from the sea. Guests on our premium trip usually stop here to swim, kayak and snorkel.

    One of the beaches approved for water activities, with forest running almost to the waterline.

    Usual activity stop on the premium route, subject to conditions.

  4. Stop 04 · The sacred curve

    Om Beach

    On our route

    Two linked crescents of sand that, seen from the hills or the sea, trace a shape often compared to the Om symbol. It is Gokarna’s best-known beach, lively by day and glowing at sunset.

    Its twin-cove outline is clearest from the water, where the whole shape comes into view at once.

    On the premium beach-hopping route.

  5. Stop 05 · Long, golden, unhurried

    Kudle Beach

    Custom trips

    A long, gentle sweep of sand between the town and Om Beach, loved for its slow pace, beach cafés and wide sunset views.

    Wide open western horizon — one of the best sunset outlooks along the coast.

    Not on the standard route. Ask us about a custom or group trip.

  6. Stop 06 · Where the temple town meets the sea

    Gokarna Main Beach

    Custom trips

    The town beach beside Gokarna’s old temple streets — a place of pilgrims, fishing boats and evening walks, with the town rising behind the sand.

    The cultural heart of Gokarna, where spiritual life and coastal life meet.

    Not on the standard route. Ask us about a custom or group trip.

Questions

Good to know before you board

Can’t find your answer? Message us and a person from our team replies.

Ask on WhatsApp

Where does the boat ride start?

All trips start from our counter near Union Bank on Tadadi Main Road, Gokarna 581326, by the Aghanashini river mouth. Om, Half Moon and Paradise Beach are reached by boat along the route.

How long are the trips and what do they cost?

The basic package (sightseeing and dolphin watching) is 1 hour at ₹799 per person. The premium beach-hopping package is 3 hours at ₹999 per person, with departures at 08:30 and 14:30. Night cruises, water sports and group trips are priced on WhatsApp.

Which places do we see?

The premium route covers the fishing harbour, Aghanashini river, the lighthouse, dolphin watching, Haven Beach, Nirvana Beach, the mystery caves, Paradise Beach, Half Moon Beach and Om Beach. The exact route depends on sea conditions on the day.

What activities are included in the premium package?

Boat ride, kayaking, swimming, sea diving and snorkeling, plus beach football, frisbee, volleyball and badminton at the beach stop. Jet ski, banana and bumper rides are not included and can be added separately.

Can we book for a group or a celebration?

Yes. We run night cruise parties for birthdays, anniversaries and new year, and offer special discounts for corporate groups and content creators. Send your group size and date on WhatsApp.

Is it suitable for families and children?

Many families with children join our trips. Tell us the ages of the children when you book and we will advise on activities and safety gear for them.

Will we see dolphins?

There is a good chance, but sightings are never guaranteed. Dolphins are wild animals and we never chase or disturb them.
